<h1 align="center">Stock Locker</h1>

<p align="center">Verified trade calls, sealed until they're due.</p>

Stock Locker lets traders prove their track record. Every trade call is timestamped and sealed the moment it's posted, then opens on a timer, so nobody can edit a call after the fact. Traders connect read-only exchange, broker and wallet accounts, and their profile shows verified holdings and realised performance. Members can follow traders for free or pay for early access to sealed calls.

### Payments

Memberships are paid on Solana with USDC, USDT or SOL through a standard [Solana Pay](https://docs.solanapay.com) transaction request. One transaction transfers each party its share (trader, platform, any affiliate), and the amount is shown before signing. Stock Locker never asks for token approvals, delegations or account-authority changes.

Our source code is private. Security researchers and wallet reviewers can request read access at admin@stocklocker.com.
